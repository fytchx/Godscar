Godscar level-1 Combat Model v1 report — raw results
Fresh Paladin, level 1, only the Common starter weapon, seeds 31 and 2026. Each bot was run up to six sim hours, or ten sim minutes after reaching stage 30. No repo simulation code was changed.
V1 was a temporary in-memory replay variant: k factor 0.00045 using the monster stage average unsplit hit; monster crit 8% at 3x. Starter gear has no crit-reduction stat, so reduction is 0%. Old-rules comparison uses the same current sim content and bot rules, but restores the old physical afterArmor formula with its 25% floor and no monster crit.
Optimizer: five-minute check-ins; passive allocation; equip if projected XP/min improves by at least 2% without more projected deaths; upgrades/salvage; choose best projected target. Lazy: 30-minute check-ins; passives; equip one best projected item; craft a Rare fire-resistance ring only when its required materials are already held; no deliberate farming.
Deaths/hour are measured in active-stage bands: 10 means highest unlocked stage 10–19; 20 means 20–29; 30 means 30–39, for ten minutes after reaching 30. Rates include ordinary and boss deaths in the band. A dash means the bot did not reach that band in six hours.
