Keep the scar as a distinct corruption contrast, not the default hue of every screen. See [VISUAL-DIRECTION-V2.md](VISUAL-DIRECTION-V2.md). Existing logo selection remains historically approved, but any revision needs review.

Status: **selected** (2026-10-30) by project owner.

## Official title treatment
The chosen wordmark is **GODSCAR**, weathered metallic gothic serif lettering, with the central O split by an intense vertical crimson dimensional scar. Transparent PNG original authored in ChatGPT: `godscar_dark_fantasy_emblem.png`; definitive 2172 x 724 RGBA, with export sizes 1400, 900, and 500 pixels wide in `Godscar_Official_Logo_Kit.zip` downloadable from the design conversation. These images must be imported into the game assets before claiming in-game integration.

## World color language
The red glow in the title represents the **same anomalous force** spreading through the Godscar, Blight-ridden terrain, portals, corrupted life and Ascension mysteries. Its brightness comes from a living fissure, not an ordinary torch/fire color. Uncorrupted Caldris environments should contrast clearly.

Illustrative UI/asset palette:
- Scar Crimson: #BC2238
- Rift Light: #F04B58
- Deep Blight: #590B19
- Ash Steel: #777077
- Forged Gold: #B89665

Do not overpower screen readability or make every interface action red. Player danger indicators must retain independent, high-contrast visibility. The wordmark is raster art, not a sprite atlas or vector font.
