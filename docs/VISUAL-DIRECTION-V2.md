# Godscar visual direction: the world beyond the wound
**Status:** owner-approved direction to BRIGHTEN Godscar on 2026-11-06 PT; specific visual execution is a proposal subject to review.

Godscar should sit between **Diablo's weight and danger** and **IdleArc's approachable clarity and readability**. We are not replacing dark fantasy with cartoon fantasy. The world should feel **worth saving, traveling through and discovering**, not like every player is trapped in the same brown-and-crimson hellscape.

## Proposed world language
- **Color before corruption:** Caldris has daylight, mossy ruins, warm stone, green valleys, blue waterways, golden autumn woods and moonlit settlements. Darkness is a *contrast*, not the entire baseline.
- **Godscar remains uncanny:** scar crimson is the visual warning of dimensional corruption and should be used selectively so its arrival has power.
- **Adventure and discovery:** varied biomes with readable materials and silhouettes; living villages, weather, ruins and skies give the player a reason to explore.
- **Threat by contrast:** corrupted zones can be deep violet, cold ash, blood red and unnatural light. Let normal lands be beautiful enough for the wound to feel wrong.
- **Items are collectible objects:** different weapon silhouettes, material classes, basetypes and effect indicators; not generic fantasy icons with random sparkles.
- **Mobile UI:** readable, high-contrast panels that needn't always use near-black. Information grouping and touch targets come before ornamental glow.

## Logo continuity
The previously approved weathered GODSCAR wordmark with crimson split O is a **historical approved identity**. The new brighter world direction may require revisiting its surrounding composition and material treatment, but **do not silently replace the owner's chosen logo** or claim that a new image is official. Official logo asset kit has not been verified in this repository.

## Proposed first environment progression
1. **The Greenward:** sunny wind-swept highlands, forgotten stone towers, battered training grounds. Promise and danger coexist. Intro safe-farm zone.
2. **Amberfall:** copperwood forests and ruined smith roads, warm earth and pale morning light. Themed item bases and forging resources.
3. **The Ashen Hollow:** corrupted crater with violet shadow and scar crimson fractures. Dangerous by deliberate contrast to the rest of the world.

These names are **working design concepts**. Existing Fallfields / Ashen Hollow gameplay and lore remain untouched pending owner direction. Existing assets need inspection before changing a real background.

## Anti-generic visual rules
Avoid gratuitous skulls, lava and saturated red everywhere; artificial neon outlines; random mismatched MMO iconography; sterile UI that could belong to any mobile gacha game. Make every location/item silhouette tied to a gameplay function and a recognizable world material.

**Implementation direction:** begin with a restrained CSS palette, friendly visual hierarchy and clearly differentiated ability/item icons in an experimental preview. Final art requires actual asset review or owner approval.
